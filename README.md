## ✨ Features
1. **Physical 3D Card Opening Experience**:
   - Deep burgundy textured card laid over ivory floral lace.
   - Circular dark burgundy wax seal with antique-gold royal `S & S` monogram.
   - Interactive unsealing animation with wax fracture, spark burst, and authentic crack sound effect.
   - 3D gatefold flaps swing open to reveal the inner card with an antique gold Lord Ganesha bas-relief and Sanskrit blessing.
2. **Sacred Wedding Announcement**:
   - Archival framed romantic photograph of Sneha D & Sooraj V with subtle parallax and gold filigree.
3. **Editorial Couple Introduction**:
   - Magazine-style portrait layout of Bride **Sneha D** and Groom **Sooraj V** featuring their actual photographs.
4. **Divine Sacred Union Invitation & Radha Krishna Artwork**:
   - Formal invitation text.
   - Classical royal painting of Lord Krishna & Radha in eternal divine love.
5. **Family Lineage**:
   - Exact family lineage, parent names, and postal addresses with one-click "Copy Address" buttons.
6. **Ceremony Details & Dual-Event Calendar**:
   - Separate event cards for the Guruvayur Temple Ceremony and Palakkad Reception.
   - Individual "Add to Calendar" (.ics file) buttons for both events.
   - Interactive Vedic rituals guide (*Ganesh Puja*, *Kanyadaanam*, *Mangalya Dharanam*, *Saptapadi*).
7. **Location & Directions**:
   - Venue coordinates, Google Maps directions buttons for both Guruvayur and Palakkad, and WhatsApp invitation sharing.
8. **Final Blessing & Guestbook**:
   - Heartfelt closing message from Sneha D & Sooraj V.
   - Quick RSVP response.
   - Interactive "Bless the Couple" guestbook with gold and rose petal confetti.
9. **Sound Design & Music Engine**:
   - Ahaana Krishna's **"Pookkal (Wedding Version)"** playing through a Web Audio analyzer with real-time visualizer equalizers.
   - Wax seal crack sound and sacred temple bell chimes on card opening.
10. **Ambient Falling Petals**:
    - Canvas-based falling jasmine and golden marigold petals with a floating toggle control.
